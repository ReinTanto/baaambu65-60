## 🎋 The Design

The Baaambu set is a commission project — a 65% and a 60% top-mount stacked laser-cut acrylic keyboard with a **bamboo log** theme, designed for streamer Baaambu. Both boards support the DZ65 and DZ60 PCBs, and the case carries a **detachable magnetic tray** on the side.

The top-mount sandwich is a unique mount specific to this case — it was not tried in any other case. The 60% was prototyped with a 2-piece PLA printed plate before the final gravy plate with flexcuts was made.

The design was modeled in 3D and rendered in November 2022. One design, two variants — 65% and 60%.
